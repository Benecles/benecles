# Master brief · PLANTA-1: each course's planta follows the professor's syllabus (CEO → orchestrator, 06/10)

**What the chairman asked.** A better look at the plantas, one per course, with the course organized more closely on the syllabus: see how the professor structured the course (units, blocks, dates, eixos, the order of topics) and make the planta express that structure, so the front reads as the professor's own map of the semester.

**What it is.** The planta is the course front's feature drawing (mandate #3: free and curated to each course, while the class register below it stays one shared instrument). Today plantas were drawn from our lesson split; this pass redraws each from the syllabus outward.

**How to build each one (WHAT):**
1. Read the course's plano/programa and Moodle structure (`~/Documents/UFRGS 2026-2`, the pipeline `course-map.md` S0 files). Write `work/plantas/<course>/structure.md`: the professor's units/blocks verbatim, dates, assessments, eixos, and the relation of our lessons to each block (one lesson may cover a block; a block may span lessons).
2. Choose a drawing genre that shows that structure's *shape* (Visual Genres v2 + Visual Casting four tests): a sequence (Latam's dated blocks and eixos), a hierarchy (Controle's 36 aulas in nine blocks), a fork (Delito's analytical concept of crime), a pipeline (Processo's procedure), a contract's life cycle (Contratos), etc. The drawing must let the reader *do* something: find where a lesson sits in the professor's plan, see what comes before it, see what the assessment covers.
3. The register (class list) below adopts the professor's grouping and labels (block headings, dates); our lesson titles sit inside the professor's blocks.
4. One desktop drawing per course (no phone variants; desktop first). Keep each course's existing colour and house paper; follow Design Direction's plate contract.

**Order:** Controle (36 aulas, nine blocks; its planta was just restored on phones), Latam (dated blocks, eixos, 05/10 activity and 30/11 exam), then Delito, Contratos, Processo, Const I, Metodologia.

**Never:** invent structure the syllabus doesn't have; drop lessons to fit the drawing; text-in-boxes diagrams (F-026); a planta that only decorates.

**Done when:** each course has `structure.md`, a redrawn planta and a register grouped by the professor's blocks; check_all + breakscan 1280 pass; the CEO gates the look course by course.

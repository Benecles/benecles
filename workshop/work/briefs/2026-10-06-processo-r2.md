# Master brief · PROC-R2: Processo Civil I rebuilt from scratch (CEO → orchestrator, 06/10)

## What this is
Direito Processual Civil I (DIR02002, Prof. Eduardo Scarparo, 2026/2) is the first course rebuilt **from zero** on everything the house has learned since it was first built: the Source Pipeline, chapter-span compendia, blueprints with a panel, the house style (marks, `.fonte` source blocks), the anti-slop gate, instruments-not-diagrams figures, the planta that follows the professor's syllabus, and a large exam-question bank. Nothing on the current Processo pages is inherited by default. The old pages, the 28/09 intake packets and the old figures are reference material: reuse a fact only after checking it against a source page.

This course becomes **the reference course**. When it's done, the other six courses are rebuilt the same way, so every step should be one we can repeat.

**What the reader gets:** a student who opens the course front sees Scarparo's semester as a procedure. Petição inicial → resposta → saneamento → prova, with the P1/P2 boundary and the readings for each week. From there they open any class and can study it in one sitting, from the assigned reading's own words, then drill on the professor's real past questions.

## The run, stage by stage (protocols/Source Pipeline.md; what changed since v1 is marked ▲)
Each item below is the WHAT; how you split, parallelise and check it is yours.

**S0 · Course map (Sol).** Write `work/pipeline/processo-civil-i/course-map.{json,md}`.
- ▲ **Lessons follow the cronograma, cut to one sitting** (mandate #4: ~2.5–4.5k words, about 15–25 min). A week whose content fills two sittings becomes two lessons (week 7, comunicação + prazos, probably does); weeks 9/11/12 overlap (saneamento runs over three weeks), so split them by concept, not by date. Every lesson records its week(s), its assigned reading, its exam (P1 / final) and its prerequisites.
- **URLs:** keep `aula-NN.html` for lessons that cover the same ground as a live page; the reader's highlights and bookmarks are keyed by path. New lessons get new numbers. Old subpages (`aula-05-casos`, `-06-citacao`, `-07-preclusao`, `-08-revelia`, `-10-estabilizacao`, `-11-merito`) either become lessons in their own right or redirect to where their content went. No dead links.
- ▲ **P2 doesn't exist on the live site yet.** Weeks 11–16 (saneamento, julgamento conforme o estado do processo, julgamentos parciais, the whole theory of evidence) have no pages. They're new lessons.

**S1 · Shelf (Luna high).** Write `shelf.csv` covering everything above.
- ▲ **Every file goes through intake the same way**, the newly added exams included: hash it, check for a text layer, OCR locally if there isn't one, verify the OCR by spot-reading against the page count.
- ▲ **Dedup:** by hash first, then by content (the same reading appears in Moodle scrape, legacy scrape and `Artigos/`). One canonical row per work, with aliases listed.
- Reuse the 28/09 OCR (Passos, Costa) rather than redoing it; note the reuse.

**S2 · Split (Luna high).** Cut each book by its own sumário into chapters with page markers (`===== p. N (PDF M) =====`). Each assigned article or chapter becomes one file. Map Didier 2015's pp. 637–670 to the 2017 edition's chapter.

**S3 · Triage (Luna xhigh).** Each chapter gets a role for each lesson: primary, supporting, background or unused, with one line of why. ▲ A lesson's **primary** material is its assigned reading first, then one treatise chapter. Never give a lesson a whole book.

**S4 · Compendium.** One workbench per lesson, assembled by script, with a provenance index. ▲ Add `60-statute.txt`: the CPC articles the lesson relies on, from the refreshed capture.

**▲ S4b · Exam bank: the chairman wants as much volume as possible.** Build `work/pipeline/processo-civil-i/exam-bank.json` (+ readable `.md`):
- **Every question from every exam file goes in**, as long as all of these hold:
  - it came from an exam (not a peer summary, not invented);
  - it belongs to the 2026/2 syllabus (a topic the cronograma lists);
  - its answer checks out against the current CPC and the compendium.
- Each entry carries:
  - source file, year, question number, verbatim text (OCR flagged as such);
  - the official answer if there is a gabarito;
  - our verified answer with its CPC article or source page;
  - topic, lesson id, and kind (objective, V/F or case).
- **Dedup at the question level.** Scarparo reuses fact patterns: 2018/2 v1 and v2 share most of their text (42 of 52 sentences). Check other years for repeats too. Same question → one entry, with every appearance listed. Same facts but a changed question → linked variants, both kept, because variants are valuable drill.
- **CPC/2015 drift:** a question written under older wording, or whose answer changed with a later amendment, is either updated with a visible "atualizada" note and the reason, or excluded with the reason. Never silently.
- **Excluded questions** go in a list with a one-line reason each, so the chairman can see nothing was dropped quietly.
- **Where the bank shows up:**
  - each lesson's closing exercises draw from its own questions first;
  - the course's revisão is the full bank, filterable by exam and topic;
  - `cartoes.html` decks are generated from the bank.

**S5 · Blueprint → panel → write (Luna max / Sol panel / Luna xhigh).** As in the Pipeline doc. Template: `protocols/templates/Lesson Blueprint.md`; reference blueprint: Controle `compendium/aula-01/blueprint.md`. The lesson pages are written **new** (this is a rebuild, not an edit of the live page). What the page must use:
- ▲ **House style** (live on Latam, rolling out on Controle; see `specimen/casa.html` and Writing Standard C10b):
  - marks: `.held` (blue, what was decided), `.limit` (orange, the limit), one `.mark` per chapter, `.term` with a `data-def` taken from the lesson's own sentence, `.runin` heads;
  - **no plain bold**;
  - every quote from a book, decision or statute is a `.fonte` block (blue = statute/decision, orange = a limit, ink = doctrine; attribution in the block header only, never in our prose);
  - our summaries of a decision are `.fonte.resumo` with a `.tese` line, never a blockquote;
  - Taruffo quotes in Italian carry a "tradução nossa".
  - The chairman wants **heavy use of source blocks**: the lesson should read as a collage of the assigned reading's own words, set into our explanation.
- ▲ **Prose:** Writing Standard Part D, D9 limits, D10 pathologies. `work/slop-bench/slop_gate.py PAGE` must PASS, and `protocols/tools/marks_lint.py PAGE` must show 0 findings. Watch Luna's measured habits (BACKTEST.md): negated inference ("não basta / não substitui…"), triads, colon reveals. No backstage: no "slides", "o professor disse", "a leitura indica", or talk about our sources outside the `.fonte` header.
- ▲ **Figures** are instruments (Visual Genres v2, Visual Casting's four tests); the same agent writes the text and its figures. Processo is procedure, so its natural instruments are timelines with deadlines (prazos counted on a real calendar), forks (which response to which situation), and the case file itself. Use the Moodle Modelos (AR, edital, mandado, certidão de NE) as the genuine documents they are: show the reader what the real thing looks like and what to look for on it. No text-in-boxes diagrams.
- **One real thread per lesson** (A5), no fictional settings (F-022). A thread from a past exam's fact pattern is ideal.

**▲ S6 · The front and the planta** (PLANTA-1 brief, `work/briefs/2026-10-06-planta-syllabus.md`).
- Write `work/plantas/processo-civil-i/structure.md` from the cronograma.
- The planta draws the semester as **the procedure itself**: one civil case moving through its phases, with Scarparo's weeks and readings placed where they sit in that procedure and the P1/P2 boundary marked. The reader finds where a class sits and what comes before it.
- The register below it groups lessons by the professor's weeks/blocks, with dates.
- Keep the course's existing colour and house paper; desktop first; Design Direction's plate contract applies.

**S7 · Revisão and cartões.** The revisão becomes the exam-bank workbench, with one block per topic (the rule, the cases, the professor's questions, the answer frame). The cartões are generated from the bank.

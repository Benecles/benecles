# P1 claim adjudications — private

## First list, question 5: laudêmio and capitalization

The supplied `RESP_1104363_PE_1263848847595.pdf` is the **earlier** Second Panel REsp 1.104.363/PE, decided 1 October 2009. It says the transfer of useful title of marine-land property to pay in company capital was gratuitous, so no laudêmio. The professor's key instead invokes **EREsp** 1.104.363/PE and selects onerous/laudêmio due. This is not an OCR error: the later Corte Especial EREsp reversed the earlier REsp. STJ Tema Repetitivo 332 confirms that later rule, citing the EREsp (DJe 2 September 2010) and REsp 1.165.276/PE (2013). Use the later rule for lesson 07 and the P1 review; never attribute it to the earlier local REsp PDF.

## A1. Reader brief
- **Already knows:** Aula 06’s distinction between action and omission and Aula 11’s objective/concentrated control; in particular, that ADPF 347 is a real case in the concentrated-control system. This lesson does not reteach either unit.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask):
  1. Given facts about a recurring rights violation, test each of the three stated ECI prerequisites and identify which one is missing, if any.
  2. Given the ADPF 347 cautelar, connect its structural diagnosis to the actual measures ordered and distinguish the 90-day institutional deadline from the 24-hour individual deadline.
  3. Given an environmental decision described in the course material, distinguish a formal ECI declaration from reasoning the slides describe as structurally analogous.
  4. Explain what a structural declaration contributes to a remedy and why it does not itself guarantee institutional implementation.
- **One sitting:** about 2.8k words, 20 min.

## A8. Cut list (and why)
- Full ADPF cabimento, subsidiarity, procedure, standing and effects doctrine → Aula 32 owns those questions; here art. 1 and art. 10 are enough to explain the declaration’s function.
- General distinctions among normative omission, ADO and mandado de injunção → Aula 31; this lesson concerns a structural condition and coordinated response, not a missing norm as such.
- Full theories of judicial activism, separation of powers, institutional dialogue and backlash → retain the tension the slides explicitly raise, but the broader debate belongs to later institutional-reaction material (Aula 18) and exceeds the assigned function.
- Detailed histories of Colombian doctrine or comparative constitutional remedies → the provided material gives only a short origin point; no comparative account is supported here.
- Detailed merits/status of later socioeducational, housing and environmental cases; prison statistics; policy proposals; whether the measures achieved compliance → the assigned source set does not establish those matters. §04 uses the later examples only for the supported label distinction.
- Repeated recap definitions and six near-duplicate quiz questions on the live page → the reader brief and three source-grounded traps provide a shorter retrieval set.

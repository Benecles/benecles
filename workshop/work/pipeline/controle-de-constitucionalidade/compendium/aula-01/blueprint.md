Written by the CEO as the S5 reference bar. Codex blueprints copy this shape.

## A1. Reader brief
- **Already known:** nothing from this course. Const I basics (what a Constitution is, art. 5º as a list of rights).
- **Can do after reading:** given a fact pattern, name the objeto, the parâmetro and a possible controlador; say why the comparison is vertical; tell a question of constitucionalidade from one of convencionalidade; tell controle from mere direct application of the CF.
- **Exam traps:** (1) "inconstitucional" used as censure, without sanction; (2) conflict between norms of the same rank (that is revogação, not inconstitucionalidade); (3) treaty under art. 5º § 3º vs supralegal treaty; (4) judge applying a right directly ≠ controle; (5) ADI at the STF only against federal/state law.
- **Scope fence:** pressupostos in depth → Aula 02; Kelsen × Schmitt → Aula 03; bloco/tratados in depth → Aulas 04–05; formal/material, ação/omissão → Aula 06; recepção → Aula 07; nulidade × anulabilidade and modulação → later. Here each gets at most one orienting sentence.

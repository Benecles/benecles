## A1. Reader brief
- **Already knows:** the lesson 02 idea that a controller must be competent; the lesson 09 distinction between preventive review and review after publication. Treat “norma já publicada” as the starting fact, not a fresh topic.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask, not topics):
  1. Given a dated enactment, decide whether review is preventive or repressivo, including a statute in vacatio legis.
  2. Given a published norm and alleged defect, choose the judicial route or the specific political/administrative response available, and name its limit.
  3. Given a decree that allegedly exceeds a statute, distinguish an ordinary-law problem from a direct constitutional defect and identify when art. 49, V is relevant.
  4. Given an audit question about a statute, state the bounded role of a Tribunal de Contas without treating Súmula 347 as blanket power to invalidate laws.
- **One sitting:** about 3.3k words, 22 minutes.

## A8. Cut list (and why)
- The detailed preventive actors, veto process, and judicial mandamus exception: Aula 09 owns the preventive route; here the only retained fact is the publication boundary needed to identify repressivo review.
- The Justice Electoral’s advisory function: outside the S0 learning outcome, and it is not a control route for the post-publication question.
- A general course overview, a full taxonomy of diffuse/concentrated systems, and the downstream effects of judicial decisions: not needed to choose among this lesson’s named actors; subsequent lessons own those details.
- Broad claims that the Executive may generally refuse to execute statutes: the sources describe controversy and bounds, so do not state a categorical doctrine.
- Claims that Súmula 347 has been revoked, or that it licenses plenary/abstract review by a Tribunal de Contas: neither is supported by the current ledger and the cited source.
- A full theory of regulatory power and all autonomous-decree cases: keep the lesson’s route-choice distinction to the decree material actually named in S0 and the IOF source example.
- The live page’s repeated summary/review blocks and its “third hypothesis: medida provisória” detour: consolidate recall into one quiz; include MP only if the S0 line or precise question requires it (neither currently does).

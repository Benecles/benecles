## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** the prerequisite `aula-06-citacao.html` teaches the reader to identify the communication act in an AR, edital, mandado, or certidão de nota de expediente. It owns the detailed rules for citation and intimation.
- **Can do after reading:**
  1. Given a request for information from another juízo, choose a cooperation route and check when the formal letter rules apply.
  2. Given a last-day filing by paper or electronically, identify the relevant cutoff and the time zone that governs it.
  3. Given a procedural act with a form, location, or electronic record, decide which CPC rule controls and identify the supported exception.
- **One sitting:** about 3,900 words, approximately 20 minutes; within the S0 planning estimate of 4,000 words and 15–25 minutes.

## A5. The thread

**Chosen:** one juízo's request that another provide information, a real cooperation mode named in CPC art. 69, caput, III. Art. 68 authorizes requests between juízos for any procedural act; art. 69 says the request is promptly answered, has no specific form, and may be executed as a request for information. The same requested act remains the carrier in every section; §05 compares it with the Code's formal-letter regime if that route is selected. No invented court, party, jurisdiction, or law carries the page.

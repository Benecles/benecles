## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** what a demand and its requests are; how art. 355 permits judgment of the whole request when the case is ready; and that the state of the process can call for judgment or further organization/instruction.
- **Can do after reading:**
  1. Read art. 356 and identify which of its two statutory grounds applies to a request or a portion of it.
  2. Distinguish a partial merits judgment from a partial grant of a request and from a different partial disposition under art. 354.
  3. Explain what is decided, what continues, why the decision is interlocutory, and what can be done with it immediately.
- **One sitting:** about 4.0k words, about 20–25 minutes.

Each task is tested in the recap: the exam's request for an example tests task 2; the two grounds test task 1; the art. 354 comparison tests task 2; and the classification/effects item tests task 3.

## A5. The thread (how it was chosen)

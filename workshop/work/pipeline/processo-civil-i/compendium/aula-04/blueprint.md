## A1. Reader task and outcome

The reader should be able to inspect a third party's asserted stake, decide whether it is juridical interest in a favorable judgment for one party, and then choose the procedural regime by asking how the judgment relates to that person's own legal relation. The lesson's single decision is: **which relation does this judgment legally affect, and whose procedural position follows from that answer?**

S0 outcome, preserved verbatim: “Identificar o interesse que permite a assistência e escolher o regime de participação do assistente.”

The necessary entry knowledge is the Aula 03 distinction among party, third party, and co-litigant. At the §02 transition, reactivate it with one retrieval cue—who must be a party, and who may enter as an assistant?—then use the answer to separate art. 114 necessity from art. 124 assistance. Do not reteach Aula 03's party or joinder classifications.

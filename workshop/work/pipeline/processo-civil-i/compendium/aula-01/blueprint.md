## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** no earlier Processo Civil I lesson is a prerequisite (`work/pipeline/processo-civil-i/course-map.json`, lesson `aula-01.html`, `prerequisites: []`). Define the procedural terms used here as they appear.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask):
  1. Read an initial petition and identify the demand, its cause of action and the requested judicial result; explain what bounds the decision.
  2. Check the items in arts. 319–320 and identify a correctable omission without treating every missing evidentiary document as an immediate bar.
  3. Classify a request as certain, determined or lawfully generic; distinguish an alternative request, alternative cumulation and subsidiary cumulation.
  4. Decide when multiple requests may be joined and calculate the stated value of the cause for the matched exam variants.
  5. Choose the rule for correcting a defect under art. 321 or changing request/cause of action under art. 329, using the procedural moment given.
- **One sitting:** approximately 4,100 words, about 25 minutes. This is within the course map’s 2.5–4.5k page range and its 15–25 minute estimate. If the draft grows, trim examples and deep cumulation doctrine before splitting the lesson.

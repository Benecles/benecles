## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** how the prior state-of-process decision asks whether instruction is still needed (Aula 10); how to distinguish the parties in a multi-party process (Aula 03); and the basic organization of the case before judgment. These are private prerequisites, not a proposed page overview.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask):
  1. Given a described ruling, identify when it leaves the merits unresolved under art. 485, rather than deciding them under art. 487.
  2. Given a no-merit disposition affecting one part of a case, decide whether the cognitive phase ended, whether the disposition is a sentence or interlocutory decision, and whether the remaining process continues.
  3. In a concrete multi-party question, separate the ruling’s reach from the number of parties in the caption.
- **One sitting:** about 3,200 words, roughly 20 minutes. The task matches S0 verbatim: “Reconhecer a saída sem resolução do mérito e distinguir o encerramento total da exclusão de uma parte do processo.”

> **Method.** These are answerable exam tasks, not topic labels. The two core tasks are tested once each in the closing quiz; the applied task is the existing 2015 P2 question, not an invented case.

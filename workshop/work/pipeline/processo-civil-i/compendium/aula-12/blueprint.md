## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** from Aula 01, a demanda has a pedido that can be identified; from Aula 03, a third party is not automatically a party to the principal demand. Use this prior knowledge without reteaching it.
- **Can do after reading**:
  1. From CPC art. 125, name the principal demand and the separate guarantee/regress claim, and identify who seeks indemnity from whom.
  2. State what happens to the regress right when denunciation is refused, omitted, or unavailable.
  3. Given the statutory role “autor” or “réu,” identify the pleading in which denunciation is requested.
  4. From arts. 128–129, determine which demand is decided next under each stated procedural condition.
  5. Distinguish the regress relation in denunciation from a direct co-obligation to the claimant.
- **One sitting:** about 3,300 words, approximately 20 minutes. This keeps S0’s 3,300-word estimate and 15–25-minute window.

Each A1 task gets one closing check in the five-item recap quiz. All quiz checks are recap-only; §05 is a worked statutory reading, not a second assessment. Each trap is explained once where it bites and tested once in the recap. The page uses only statutory wording and sourced doctrinal propositions; it invents no parties, dispute, judgment, dates, or case facts.

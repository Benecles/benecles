## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** Aula 14's role of proof in justification; Aula 15's delimitation of the fact and admissibility of the evidentiary means. The page may use those terms without re-teaching their foundations.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask, not topics):
  1. Separate an allegation, evidentiary material, the reason assigned to it, and the conclusion drawn from it in a short decision excerpt.
  2. Explain what CPC art. 371 requires of an account of valuation, and identify how arts. 372, 479, and 489 sharpen that requirement in their settings.
  3. Distinguish the model of proof used to decide a disputed fact from the burden rule that allocates the consequence of unresolved proof.
  4. Given a source-bounded record, state what it supports, what standard question remains, and what cannot be inferred from the excerpt alone.
  5. Locate and answer the mapped Q9 item; recognize that the bank's legal rationale needs source material absent from this compendium before it can be taught as a supported explanation.
- **S0 learning outcome (verbatim):** “Avaliar o suporte probatório de uma conclusão e distinguir valoração do padrão exigido para decidir.”
- **One sitting:** 3,700 words; 15–25 minutes, per S0. One page; no split proposed.

## A3. Scope fence (PLANNING ONLY, NEVER ON THE PAGE)

- Proof's general function, truth, object, and basic right/duty to prove → Aula 14. Aula 16 uses only the distinctions needed to evaluate support and the decision threshold.
- Fixing the fact to be proved and the general admissibility of means → Aula 15. Aula 16 uses admissibility only to distinguish “may this material enter?” from “what does it support?” and to locate Q9.
- Dynamic allocation of the burden → Aula 17. Aula 16 uses CPC art. 373 only to explain the consequence of unresolved proof and the distinction from the model of proof.
- Presumptions, maxims of experience, and inferences → Aula 18. Aula 16 does not develop those doctrines.

Reader-facing copy may use one brief boundary sentence with a link only where a reader would otherwise need the prerequisite. The page must contain no production notes, source inventories, slide references, or mentions of this fence.

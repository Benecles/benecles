## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** citação and intimação as communication modes (Aula 06-citação); procedural acts, time and electronic practice (Aula 06).
- **Can do after reading** (the S0 outcome is copied verbatim):
  1. **Fixar o termo inicial, contar o prazo e identificar quando o poder de praticar o ato se encerra.**
  2. Given a record plus the governing period rule and applicable calendar, locate the triggering event, first counted day and final day; where any input is absent, name that input before asserting a date.
  3. For a sourced final day, state the consequence art. 223 attaches if the act was not performed, while distinguishing that conditional consequence from a record that proves actual nonperformance and preserving the statutory just-cause exception.
  4. Classify expiry, prior exercise and incompatible conduct as temporal, consumptive or logical preclusion, with one matching closing item for each.
- **One sitting:** target 4,000 words, 20–25 minutes including figure and closing questions.

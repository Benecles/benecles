## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** citação and intimação as communication modes (Aula 06-citação); procedural acts, time and electronic practice (Aula 06).
- **Can do after reading** (the S0 outcome is copied verbatim):
  1. **Fixar o termo inicial, contar o prazo e identificar quando o poder de praticar o ato se encerra.**
  2. Given an actual record, identify the act, applicable procedural code, triggering event and term; calculate its due date from the governing rule and calendar.
  3. Given a calculated final day, state the legal effect of expiry conditionally, without treating the record as proof that the act was missed.
  4. Distinguish temporal preclusion from loss of a power already exercised and from loss caused by incompatible conduct.
- **One sitting:** target 4,000 words, 20–25 minutes including the figure and closing questions.

## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** the initial-petition-to-hearing sequence (Aula 02) and the roles of parties in the process (Aula 03). No assumption that the reader can calculate a deadline.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask, not topics):
  1. Read an AR, edital, mandado or Nota de Expediente certificate and name the communication act recorded.
  2. Separate a completed citation from a failed attempt, and identify which sentence in the record supports that reading.
  3. State what a record proves, what it leaves uncertain, and when the current CPC rule differs from an older specimen.
  4. Explain the statutory conditions for hora certa without treating one unsuccessful visit as enough.
- **One sitting:** about 3,800 words, 20–25 minutes. This stays inside S0's 4,000-word and 15–25-minute target.

Scope fence (PLANNING ONLY, NEVER ON THE PAGE)

- `Comunicação dos atos`, `Intimação` and `Citação` are this lesson's direct scope; cover the statutory function and identification of the assigned communication records.
- `Processo eletrônico` is covered only to explain current electronic citation under art. 246 and the point at which the statute moves to another citation route. The broader electronic-process workflow belongs to `aula-06.html`, “Atos processuais, processo eletrônico e cooperação.”
- `Cartas` and `Cooperação Nacional` belong to `aula-06.html`; the assigned outcome is to identify a communication act, not select a letter or plan inter-court cooperation.
- General `Atos processuais`, their `Tempo e lugar`, and the broader treatment of communication by letter also belong to `aula-06.html`. Do not teach the taxonomy of acts, territorial rules or place-of-performance doctrine here.
- `Prazos` belong to `aula-07.html` (“Prazos e preclusão”). The Nota de Expediente artifact's dates may be read as recorded; do not calculate a response deadline. At most one natural link to Aula 07 may appear where the reader asks what happens after identifying the publication date.
- The live route stays `aula-06-citacao.html`; no scope-fence language, course-map references or statements such as “nesta aula veremos” appear on the page.

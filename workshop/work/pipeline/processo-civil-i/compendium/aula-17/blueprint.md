## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** how to identify the facts to be proved, distinguish admissibility from evaluation, and read evidentiary support against a decision standard.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask, not topics):
  1. Given a fact asserted in a claim or defense, classify it under art. 373 and identify the ordinary burden-bearer; distinguish that allocation from contraprova on the other side’s fact.
  2. Explain how the same rule guides the parties during instruction and allocates the risk if the judge remains uncertain at the end.
  3. Given a proposed redistribution, test its legal basis, state the reason that supports it, preserve an opportunity to meet the new burden, and reject a transfer that would make proof impossible or excessively difficult.
- **One sitting:** approximately 3,300 words, 18–20 minutes, within S0’s 15–25-minute estimate.

## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** the process-party and joinder vocabulary introduced in Aula 03 and the comparison with denunciação expected by S0's Aula 12 prerequisite. Do not reteach either topic.
- **Can do after reading** (each task is tested below):
  1. Given a procedural purpose, select chamamento, IDPJ, or amicus curiae by the relationship and result sought.
  2. Given a defendant sued for a common debt, identify whether art. 130 permits calling the named co-obligor, when the request is made, and what the judgment permits after payment.
  3. Given a request to reach a separate patrimony, state who may request the IDPJ, when the incident is dispensed with, and what participation follows.
  4. Given a proposed amicus, apply art. 138's admission criteria and state who fixes the participant's powers and which appeals remain possible.
- **One sitting:** ~3,600 words, about 20 minutes. No split: the three rules answer one comparative task and fit one sitting.

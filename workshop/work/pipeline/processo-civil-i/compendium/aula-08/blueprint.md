## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** how the initial demand frames the dispute (Aula 01), how the hearing fits before the response (Aula 02), and how to count a procedural deadline once its first day is known (Aula 07).
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask):
  1. Given a procedural history, select the event that starts the ordinary contestation period and apply the relevant duration for the defendant.
  2. Put procedural preliminaries, merits defenses, facts, legal grounds, and intended evidence in the right place in one contestation.
  3. Given an allegation from the initial pleading, identify the consequence of leaving it unanswered and the statutory exceptions.
  4. Distinguish a timely supplement from a new allegation after the contestation period, using the rule and its limits.
- **One sitting:** about 3,700 words, approximately 20–25 minutes.

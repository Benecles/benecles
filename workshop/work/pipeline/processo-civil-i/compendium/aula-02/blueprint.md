## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** from Aula 01, how the initial petition delimits the demand and the difference between request and cause of action (`aula-01.html`; carried forward in `40-background-001-didier-curso-vol1-2017-ch15-s01.txt`, PDF pp. 615–649, and `40-background-002-didier-curso-vol1-2017-ch15-s02.txt`, PDF pp. 650–664). Do not reteach initial-petition requirements.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks, each tested below):
  1. Given a question about an early disposition, distinguish a procedural indeferimento from an improcedência that resolves the merits and state the future consequence identified by Q8 (quiz item 1).
  2. Apply the separate statutory conditions for improcedência liminar without treating any unfavorable or precedent-related request as sufficient (quiz item 2).
  3. Place Art. 334's three timing marks—30 days for designation, 20 for defendant citation, and 10 for the defendant's disinterest petition—on the hearing-relative scale and label the actor/act at each mark (docket figure and quiz 3a).
  4. Given the statutory conditions, select the immediate act: Art. 332 judgment when its predicates are met; otherwise the Art. 334 hearing unless a §4 exception applies (quiz 3b).
- **One sitting:** about 3.8k words, one page, approximately 20–25 minutes. No split: the two statutory routes are one initial-stage decision, and separating them would hide the required handoff.

> **Method:** Each outcome is phrased as something an exam can ask the reader to do. The Q8 exam prompt is the worked case; each other task is tested once in the closing quiz or the figure.

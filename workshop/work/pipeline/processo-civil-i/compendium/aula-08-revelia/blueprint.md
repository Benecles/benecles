## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** the demand and requested relief from Aula 01; the structure and deadline of contestação from Aula 08; the basic count and timing of procedural periods from Aula 07.
- **Can do after reading:**
  1. Given a defendant’s filing, tell whether it resists the plaintiff’s demand or asserts a connected demand of the defendant’s own; explain how each demand can be decided.
  2. Given no contestation by the deadline, distinguish the procedural status of revelia from the possible presumption about factual allegations and identify the four statutory exceptions.
  3. Given a revel defendant with or without counsel, state how deadlines run, when the defendant may enter, and when proof may still be produced.
  4. Resolve the exact 2018/2 P1 v2 Q3 facts about Florinda without turning that item into a general lesson on service by hora certa or special curatorship.

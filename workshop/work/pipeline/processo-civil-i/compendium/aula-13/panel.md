## Required changes

1. **Replace or reframe the carrier before S5.** The excerpt identifies the case as a Juizado Especial Cível judgment dated **15 January 2016**, and its operative text says only that the judge recognized decadence and extinguished the action. It does not cite Art. 354 or Art. 487 II. The CPC/2015 took effect only after a year from its official publication, and its Art. 1.046 §2 preserves special statutory procedures with supplementary CPC application. Lei 9.099/1995 Arts. 27–29 prescribe their own hearing and incident sequence. Accordingly, A4 #7/#12, A5, A6 §§05–06, and Fig. 3 must not say this *actual ruling* followed CPC/2015 Art. 354 or that the case actually moved through Arts. 347–357. Prefer a real, adequately sourced post-vigência ordinary-procedure record that can carry the same six questions. If none is available, keep this historical Juizado excerpt only as a clearly bounded contrast and choose another real current-law thread; recast the figure claims and every affected locator. Sources checked: `30-supporting-002-moodle-model-julgamento-sinceridade-moodle-model-julgamento-sinceridade.txt`; `60-statute.txt`; [CPC Arts. 1.045–1.046](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2015/lei/l13105compilada.htm); [Lei 9.099/1995 Arts. 27–29](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9099.htm).
2. **Qualify the disputed-date variation.** A disputed purchase date alone does not establish that instruction is required; the factual dispute must be material to the decision and still require proof. In A4 #10, A6 §06, and Fig. 3's card, condition the Art. 357 route on that need, using Art. 355 I and Art. 357 II from `60-statute.txt`. Keep the hypothetical change visibly distinct from the actual record.
3. **Make the final test cover the whole function.** Add or revise closing items so the reader must distinguish an Art. 352 maximum from a mandatory period and identify the five-day request window and stability rule in Art. 357 §1, while retaining one test per T1–T3. Update A1/A2/A6 together so the item map and word budget remain coherent. Both rules are in `60-statute.txt`.

This is the first REVISE for this blueprint. The current flight log's F-023b supersedes the old general S5 pause; approval of this blueprint remains a separate Source Pipeline gate.

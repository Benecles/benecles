## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** contestation, revelia and the basic vocabulary for identifying a procedural defect (the S0 prerequisites: Aulas 08, 08-Revelia and 09). Do not re-teach those topics or the full taxonomy of invalidity.
- **Can do after reading** (tasks a question could ask):
  1. Given a case file after the response period, identify which preliminary measures the current CPC requires before the next decision, without treating the 2016 Juizado excerpt as proof that those provisions governed its event.
  2. Given a proposed change after citation, distinguish a change to the request/cause of action from a new factual assertion and apply Art. 329’s timing and consent conditions.
  3. Given an irregular act, state Passos’s relevance test as his theory, identify the current CPC correction route, and state Art. 352’s maximum period.
  4. Given a disputed purchase date, decide whether it is material and still requires proof; distinguish Art. 355 I from Art. 357 II after checking the available judgment routes.
  5. State when the Art. 357 saneamento order becomes stable after the parties’ opportunity to request clarification or adjustments.
- **One sitting:** about 3,200 words, approximately 20 minutes. This follows the S0 estimate.

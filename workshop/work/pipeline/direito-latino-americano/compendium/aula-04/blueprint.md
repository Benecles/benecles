## A1. Reader brief

- **Already knows:** Aula 02's distinction between courts and their modes of constitutional review; Aula 03's STF ADPF 153, Gomes Lund, amnesty, truth, justice and reparation. The reader can distinguish a national constitutional court from the Corte IDH before the first case. Test this distinction **once in §01** with “which tribunal, which decision, which legal object?”; do not keep announcing it.
- **Can do after reading:**
  1. Given art. 66 transitional, separate **prioritization** (order of investigation) from **selection/conditional renunciation** (scope of criminal pursuit), and state the limits C-579 imposed.
  2. Given an excerpt of C-694's `resuelve`, distinguish a merits holding from `inhibida` and `estarse a lo resuelto`, and explain why prioritization coexists with victims' participation.
  3. Given a civilian, a person with constitutional forum, a tutela, or a foreign jurist in the JEP scheme, apply C-674's particular boundary without calling the JEP invalid as a whole.
  4. Write an eixo answer with all three Colombian cases and the Brazilian comparison: court/year/object/result/reasons/dissent/what followed.
- **One sitting:** 3,900–4,200 Portuguese words, roughly 25–28 minutes. Never reduce below the live count merely to fit a template.

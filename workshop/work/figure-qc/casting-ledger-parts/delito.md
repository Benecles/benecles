### Repeats

- **Course navigation strip — deliberate callback:** all `*-s1` navigation strips repeat the same cascade form to locate a different lesson in the course; this is the single deliberate course-wide callback, not a repeated instructional figure.
- **Decision paths/forms — different moves:** `tdl-u01-s2+4` / `tdl-u12-s2+3` / `tdl-u12-erro-proibicao-s2+3` / `tdl-u10-s2+3` respectively filter the crime’s stages, assess capacity, classify a prohibition error, and screen justification grounds; `tdl-u15-s2+4` and `tdl-u13-casos-s2+3` apply person-specific participation and Article 22 tests. The shared branching form serves distinct questions.
- **Case records — different moves:** `tdl-u01-casos-s2+4`, `tdl-u09-casos-s2+3`, `tdl-u05-s5+2`, and `tdl-u15-casos-s2+2` place cases at decisive filters, establish permission timing, classify an identical-backpack mistake, and compare attributed conduct by person.
- **Trees/sequences — different moves:** `tdl-u02-s2+3`, `tdl-u01-s8`, and `tdl-u08-s2+3` show model genealogy, consequences of compulsion, and the progress from project to consummation.
